In and out of Pasteur’s quadrant: revisiting trends in corporate science

with Francesco Lissoni

Industry and Innovation, 2025.

We examine long-term changes in the nature of US corporate scientific research. Using measures of basicness and appliedness, we find that corporate science has progressively become more applied and less basic relative to academic research, particularly among large established firms.

Working papers

Corporate Science and IPOs

Federico Bignone

I study the causal effect of going public on firms' scientific activity by comparing completed and withdrawn IPOs. The results show that going public increases scientific output and collaborations, with evidence that capital raised and the inflow of scientists contribute to this effect.

Academy of Management Proceedings

US Corporate Science Data: An Algorithm for Linking Web of Science to Bureau van Dijk Orbis

with Russell Thomson

This project develops a large-scale linkage between scientific publications and company data, allowing researchers to identify scientific publications produced by US firms and analyse the changing organisation of corporate science.
